## Yin-Chun Lin

**Postdoctoral Researcher**  
National Academy for Educational Research, Taiwan  

Ph.D. in Technical and Vocational Education  
National Taipei University of Technology  

Former Visiting Scholar  
The Pennsylvania State University  

---

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the **National Academy for Educational Research (NAER), Taiwan**, working at the intersection of **education, science and innovation studies, and computational social science**.

My research examines how **institutions, policies, and collaboration networks shape the production, diffusion, and distribution of knowledge and opportunity**. I am particularly interested in using large-scale empirical data to understand how educational and research systems operate, how inequalities emerge across institutions and populations, and how policy environments influence these processes.

My research currently spans two closely connected areas. The first focuses on **education policy and international comparative education**, including international large-scale assessments, educational inequality, and cross-national comparisons. The second focuses on **higher education, science policy, and innovation systems**, particularly scientific knowledge production, international research collaboration, university–industry linkages, and the relationship between academic research and technological innovation.

Methodologically, I work with large-scale datasets including **international education data, scientific publications, patents, and institutional data**. My research combines **econometric analysis, bibliometrics, social network analysis, spatial analysis, time-series methods, and natural language processing**.

Across these areas, my broader goal is to connect **rigorous empirical analysis with substantive questions in education, science, and innovation policy**, using computational methods not simply as technical tools, but as a way to uncover patterns and mechanisms that are difficult to observe through conventional approaches.
